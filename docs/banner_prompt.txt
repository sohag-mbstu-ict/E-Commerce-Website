Tips
If the subject ends up on the wrong side, reply: Move the subject to the right third of the frame and keep the left half empty.
If text or letters appear in the image, reply: Remove all text and letters from the image.
Keep the banners consistent: use the same lighting line and dark left side for all five, so the slider looks like one campaign.
On phones the slider is narrow, so part of the left and right edges gets cut off. Keeping the subject compact in the right third (not touching the edge) means it stays visible.
The background colors in each prompt match the slide's current gradient (red, purple, gold, dark slate), so the banners blend with the existing design even before the image field is added.
